## Course outline (from the slides)

Introduction to basic computation of NLP: language model, tokenization, n-gram, word embedding, part-of-speech tagging, sequence labelling, recurrent neural language model, encoder-decoder model, attention model, syntax trees and parsing.

**CLOs:** (1) explain the basic principles of natural language processing; (2) choose NLP technologies appropriately for a task.

**References:** Raymond Lee (2023), *Natural Language Processing: A Textbook with Python Implementation*; Jurafsky and Martin (2026), *Speech and Language Processing*, 3rd ed. (online manuscript, web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3).
